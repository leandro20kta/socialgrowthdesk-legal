SocialGrowthDesk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

SocialGrowthDesk is a personal, single-user desktop tool used by the owner of a YouTube channel. It has one user: the channel owner, who is also its developer. It is not offered to the public and has no server operated by the developer.

Google user data accessed

Only with the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app requests these scopes for the owner's own YouTube channel:

  • youtube.readonly – read channel and video information.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – read the channel's own analytics.
  • youtube.force-ssl – read comments on the channel's videos and, only when the owner turns the reply agent on or approves a reply, post replies from the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.upload – upload a video to the owner's own channel, only when the owner starts and approves an upload.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show comments and statistics, draft or post replies chosen by the owner, upload videos the owner chooses, and suggest video ideas. Everything is stored locally on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Windows DPAPI for the owner's Windows account. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with other people.

AI processing

Comment replies and video ideas are generated by an AI model that runs locally on the same computer. Separately, the owner can enable an optional planning assistant that sends text the owner writes (briefs, captions) and aggregated statistics of the owner's own channel to Anthropic through the owner's own Claude Code session. Video files, tokens and credentials are never sent. Google user data obtained through the YouTube APIs is not used to develop or train generalized AI models.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted by removing the app's local data folder.

Política de Privacidade (resumo em português)

Ferramenta pessoal de um único utilizador, sem servidor próprio. Com consentimento OAuth do dono do canal, lê canal, estatísticas e comentários do próprio canal, publica respostas só quando o dono liga o agente ou aprova, e envia vídeos só quando o dono o inicia. Os dados e os tokens (cifrados com DPAPI) ficam apenas no computador do dono; nada é vendido nem partilhado. As respostas e ideias usam IA local; um assistente de planeamento opcional pode enviar à Anthropic, pela sessão do próprio dono, textos que ele escreve e estatísticas agregadas do seu canal, nunca vídeos nem credenciais. O acesso pode ser revogado em myaccount.google.com/permissions.
