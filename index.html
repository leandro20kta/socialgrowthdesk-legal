SocialGrowthDesk

SocialGrowthDesk is a personal, single-user desktop tool that helps the owner of a YouTube channel read and reply to comments on that same channel, view the channel's own analytics, upload the owner's own videos, and plan new video ideas. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

It uses the YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs through Google OAuth. Replies are posted from the owner's own channel only when the owner turns the reply agent on or approves them.

Privacy Policy

Português: ferramenta pessoal, de um único utilizador, que corre apenas no computador do dono de um canal do YouTube para ler e responder a comentários do próprio canal, ver as suas estatísticas, enviar os seus vídeos e planear ideias. Não é oferecida ao público.
